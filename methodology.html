How MMA Math Engine v5 Works

Engine v5 pairs an independent quantitative fighter model with sharp market benchmarks and 1,000-run fight simulations—designed for long-term calibration and bankroll discipline without exposing proprietary weights or formulas.

1. 20-Signal Fighter Evaluation

Pure 0%-Market Projection

Every fighter is evaluated on opponent-adjusted striking and grappling efficiency, recent form, strength of schedule, physical matchup leverage, durability, and inactivity—completely independent of betting odds.

2. Sample-Aware Market Comparison

Beating Retail Juice

Pure projections are compared against devigged prediction-market prices and sportsbook lines. Thin-sample prospects automatically lean more on sharp market consensus, while proven UFC veterans rely heavily on statistical edge.

3. Directional Finish Simulation

1,000-Run Fight Scripts

Instead of generic fight finish rates, Engine v5 separates each fighter's offensive finishing threat from their opponent's defensive stoppage vulnerability across 1,000 round-by-round simulations to project KO/TKO, Submission, and Decision splits.

4. Strict Unit Discipline

100u Bankroll Protection

Every play is sized on a standardized 100u scale using fractional Kelly staking, strict favorite-price caps, and minimum +EV thresholds—automatically flagging overpriced favorites as AVOID or PASS.
