How MMA Math Engine v5.1 Works

Engine v5.1 pairs an independent quantitative fighter model with sharp market benchmarks and 1,000-run fight simulations—designed for long-term calibration and bankroll discipline without exposing proprietary weights or formulas.

1. 20-Signal Fighter Evaluation

Independent Fighter Model

Every fighter is evaluated on opponent-adjusted striking and grappling efficiency, recent form, strength of schedule, physical matchup leverage, durability, and inactivity—completely independent of betting odds.

2. Sample-Aware Market Comparison

Finding Value vs. Bookmakers

Pure projections are compared against devigged prediction-market prices and sportsbook lines. Thin-sample prospects automatically lean more on sharp market consensus, while proven UFC veterans rely heavily on statistical edge.

3. Directional Finish Simulation

1,000-Run Fight Simulations

Instead of generic fight finish rates, Engine v5.1 separates each fighter's offensive finishing threat from their opponent's defensive stoppage vulnerability across 1,000 round-by-round simulations to project KO/TKO, Submission, and Decision splits.

4. Strict Unit Discipline

Bankroll Protection (100u Scale)

Every play is sized on a standardized 100u scale using fractional Kelly staking, strict favorite-price caps, and minimum +EV thresholds—automatically flagging overpriced favorites as AVOID or PASS.

Verified Pre-Fight Testing

  • Zero Future Leakage: Trained on 7,562 fights (2010–2024) and tested strictly out-of-sample on 935 unseen fights (Jan 2025 – Aug 2026) using only pre-fight stats and fight-night ages.
  • High-Conviction Separation: On unseen historical slates without betting odds, Engine v5.1 clears 70%+ accuracy in its top-conviction tier while passing on coin-flip variance.
  • Public Live Grading: Every pick on the Road to 100 Fights (currently 18–6–1 · 75.0%) is timestamped pre-fight and graded against official UFC results.

Monte Carlo Simulation Limitations

Our 1,000-run round simulations estimate KO/Sub/Decision distributions from historical fighter rates. They cannot predict mid-fight freak injuries, illegal fouls, weight-miss stamina crashes not captured pre-fight, or outlier judging scorecards.
